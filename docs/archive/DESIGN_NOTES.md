# Maison Botanique · floral direction

This publicly viewable review storefront is a design and shopping-flow concept for Dubai gifting. Maison Botanique is the confirmed brand; the imagery, copy, prices and catalog remain provisional.

## Design decisions

- The opening acts like an editorial still life. A bouquet, gift arrangement and preserved rose each move through one pinned scene as the visitor scrolls. The sequence ends naturally and releases the page.
- Warm plaster, deep olive, dusty blush and a measured oxblood accent give the flowers their own visual space. Cormorant Garamond lends the French-leaning editorial voice; DM Sans handles the practical controls, with Noto Naskh Arabic for RTL.
- The hero leads directly into a horizontal collection. Seven distinct provisional photographs appear in staggered crops, with quiet captions and a visible next piece. The cards are sized to show about three photographs together on a typical desktop viewport and a clear glimpse of the next piece on a phone. Native horizontal scrolling, previous/next controls and a progress indicator keep exploration clear.
- The catalog can be browsed by type or occasion and searched in English or Arabic. A single product record powers every view; each photograph opens product details and an add-to-bag path. Filters stay exposed on phones.
- The final section explains the actual gifting path with a compact three-step disclosure. It describes the gift note and preferred date already available in the product sheet without promising delivery terms that have not been approved.
- Reduced-motion visitors get a static gallery with previous/next controls. English and Arabic flows share the same product and cart state.

## Reference study

- [Maison Lachaume](https://maisonlachaume.com/en/collections/fleurs): heritage floral restraint and white space.
- [FLOWERBX](https://www.flowerbx.com/ae/floral-concierge): flowers presented as considered gifts.
- [Wild at Heart](https://wildatheart.com/): large-scale editorial floral storytelling.
- [Venus et Fleur](https://www.venusetfleur.com/): preserved flowers treated as display objects.
- [GSAP ScrollTrigger](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/): pin and scrub implementation reference.

These are directional references, not source material to copy. The site uses original layout, copy and provisional concept imagery.
