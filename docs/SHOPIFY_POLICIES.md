# Store policies for Shopify

Shopify's payment review checks that customers can find these at checkout. Paste each text under **Settings → Policies** in the Shopify admin. They match the website (`/refund-policy`, `/terms`, `/delivery`, `/contact`). If you change one, change the other.

Contact email on both: **maisonbotaniqueflowers@gmail.com**. The business address entered in Shopify (Settings → General) should be the one shown on the contact page; it is not on the website yet.

## Refund policy

If your flowers arrive damaged or not as described, send us your request within 30 minutes of delivery. Requests made after that cannot be accepted.

Write to maisonbotaniqueflowers@gmail.com with your order number and a photograph of the flowers.

If your request is accepted, we will replace the arrangement or refund the order to your original payment method, and we will confirm which by email.

To change or cancel an order, write to maisonbotaniqueflowers@gmail.com as early as possible. Fresh flowers are prepared especially for you, so we may not be able to cancel an order once it has been arranged.

## Shipping policy

We deliver same day across the UAE. Delivery is AED 30 in Dubai and AED 50 elsewhere in the UAE. Enter the full recipient address at checkout, including building or villa, apartment number and area.

Choose the delivery day and a three-hour window (9am to 9pm) in your bag, today or up to 60 days ahead. Same-day windows stay open until three hours before they start. One delivery per order.

If no one is available to receive the flowers, we will contact you to arrange the next step.

## Terms of service

Use the text of the website's Terms page (`/terms`): orders, natural variation, delivery, changes and cancellations, and the refund rule above.

## Contact information

Email: maisonbotaniqueflowers@gmail.com. Add the business phone number and address once confirmed.
