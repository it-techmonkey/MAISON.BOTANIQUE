## Refund policy

If your flowers arrive damaged or not as described, send us your request within 30 minutes of delivery. Requests made after that cannot be accepted.

Write to hello@maisonbotaniqueflowers.com with your order number and a photograph of the flowers.

We reply by email once we have seen your photograph. If your request is accepted, we will either replace the arrangement or refund the order, and we will confirm which by email.

A refund covers the price of the affected arrangement and is paid to the original payment method, in UAE dirhams. Your bank or card provider may take several working days to show it on your statement.

We cannot refund an order for a change of mind, for natural variation in seasonal flowers, or because the address was incomplete or no one was available to receive the delivery. If a delivery could not be made, write to us and we will help arrange the next step.

To change or cancel an order, write to hello@maisonbotaniqueflowers.com as early as possible. Fresh flowers are prepared especially for you, so we may not be able to cancel an order once it has been arranged.

## Shipping policy

We deliver same day across the UAE. Delivery is AED 30 in Dubai and AED 50 elsewhere in the UAE. Enter the full recipient address at checkout, including building or villa, apartment number and area.

Choose the delivery day and a three-hour window (9am to 9pm) in your bag, today or up to 60 days ahead. Same-day windows stay open until three hours before they start. One delivery per order.

If no one is available to receive the flowers, we will contact you to arrange the next step.

## Terms of service

**Orders.** An order is confirmed when you receive our confirmation email. Prices are shown in UAE dirhams and the total, including any delivery fee, is shown at checkout before you pay.

**Natural variation.** Every arrangement is made by hand with seasonal flowers. Colours, stems and wrapping may vary slightly from the photographs, and we may substitute a flower of equal quality when needed.

**Delivery.** Please make sure the recipient's address and phone number are correct. If no one is available to receive the flowers, we will contact you to arrange the next step. Delivery fees and times are in our Shipping policy.

**Changes and cancellations.** If you need to change or cancel an order, write to hello@maisonbotaniqueflowers.com as early as possible. Fresh flowers are prepared especially for you, so we may not be able to cancel an order once it has been arranged.

**If something is not right.** If your flowers arrive damaged or not as described, send us your request within 30 minutes of delivery. Our Refund policy explains what we will do.
